## Финальный промпт

Use case: ads-marketing.
Generate a polished Open Graph social sharing cover for the existing "Audio To Text Transcription" Chrome extension website.
Landscape aspect ratio 1.90476:1, target exactly 1200 x 630 pixels if supported. All essential content inside generous 70px safe margins.
Brand visual language: bright white / very pale ice blue background, near-black modern sans-serif typography, rich royal blue #2563EB and gentle cyan accents matching a clean professional browser productivity tool.
Composition: strong highly legible large two-line headline on the left, a small blue rounded microphone symbol near the product identity. On the right a refined conceptual illustration of blue audio waveform smoothly becoming an orderly transcript document with blue text-like lines and small timestamp-like geometric marks. This is a conceptual illustration, NOT a fake product screenshot. Restrained soft shadows, crisp edges, plenty of white space, confident editorial hierarchy. No people, no stock-photo desk, no Chrome or Google logo, no invented certifications or reviews, no accuracy percentages, no promises about real-time captions, no pricing, no watermarks.
Render ONLY these exact three text elements, perfectly spelled in English:
Large headline: "Audio to Text"
Second headline line: "Transcription"
Small supporting label: "Chrome extension"
Do not add any other words, URL, tiny UI text or fake transcript paragraphs. Readable at small link-preview size. A finished full-bleed bitmap image, not a mockup sitting on a canvas or device.
